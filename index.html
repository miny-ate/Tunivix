Company Overview

Industrial supply today. Manufacturing tomorrow.

Our ambition is a leading East African industrial enterprise, with a strong supply network and, progressively, our own manufacturing capability.

Tunivix Industries Limited supplies quality construction materials, industrial products and infrastructure-related solutions to corporate organisations, contractors, developers, government institutions and other large-scale projects.

We work through strategic manufacturing partnerships, competitive pricing and efficient distribution, and we are building toward fabrication and manufacturing of our own.
