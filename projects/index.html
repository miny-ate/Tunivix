Roadmap

From supply to manufacturing, 2027 and beyond.

Tunivix is built around three pillars. We start with supply and distribution, and move into fabrication and manufacturing as the business grows.

Strategic Development

Five phases of growth.

2027

Establish supply operations and secure corporate contracts

2028

Expand product portfolio and distribution network

2029

Secure major institutional and infrastructure supply contracts

2030

Establish fabrication and manufacturing facilities

2031 onwards

Expand manufacturing and distribution across East Africa

Three Pillars

The foundation of the business.

Supply

Construction and infrastructure materials, supplied through bulk procurement, tenders and framework agreements.

Distribution

Industrial products and manufacturer partnerships, including authorised distribution and importation.

Manufacturing

Steel fabrication, structural metal products and our own proprietary products and brands.
