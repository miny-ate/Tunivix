About Tunivix

A Kenyan industrial supplier with manufacturing ambitions.

Tunivix Industries Limited supplies the construction, engineering, manufacturing and infrastructure sectors, and is building toward a manufacturing capability of its own.

An industrial supply and development enterprise, not merely a hardware retailer or construction contractor.

Tunivix Industries Limited is a Kenyan industrial supply and infrastructure materials company, established to serve construction, engineering, manufacturing and infrastructure development.

We supply quality construction materials, industrial products and infrastructure-related solutions to corporate organisations, contractors, developers, government institutions and other large-scale projects.

Our long-term ambition is to evolve from a distributor of industrial and construction materials into an established manufacturer, with our own products, production facilities and regional distribution network.
