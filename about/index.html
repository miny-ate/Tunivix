About Tunivix

Engineers and fabricators, building for the long run.

Tunivix Industries Limited brings engineering, fabrication and manufacturing together under one roof — so a project moves from drawing board to delivery without losing precision along the way.

Practical engineering, made to last — that's the standard we hold every project to.

We work across Kenya, East Africa and international markets, serving clients who need technical solutions they can rely on — from structural design and precision metalwork to full production runs.

Our team combines hands-on fabrication experience with engineering discipline, so every piece of work is designed properly before it's ever cut, welded or assembled.

Whether it's a single custom component or an ongoing manufacturing contract, we hold the same standard: built right, on schedule, to specification.
